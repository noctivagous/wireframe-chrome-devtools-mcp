Reflow Plain Text To Do

- issue: sometimes words are conjoined (the space
between them deleted) after reflow.

- modes: absolute cutoff flow feature, edge-protruding,
justified (hyphenated cutoffs), justified (adds spaces). 
for edge-protruding: flow width number as number with 
tolerance level added instead of absolute
cutoff.  So,  if the tolerance is 5 and the
char. width is 40, it will allow words that
fit inside 45 characters.  so if there is a word
that is 5 char long it will be allowed but
1 + space + 3 char will be taken to the next line.

- slider for char. width sits on text area, knob is
triangle pointing downward, and point of triangle
lines up with right margin.

- text reflowed as soon as it is pasted in.

- text reflowed at end of slider move event.

- Feature: "left margin" slider:  (default: 0 char.)

- Feature: "Frame": all edges unicode blocks with character margin.
Left column of solid blocks.  inner margin: 1 char. (default)
frame type: [solid blocks, singe line, double line]

- Feature: Right and left edge gradients (either side) 
using unicode blocks. gap: 1 character (default).
left margin from edge gradient: 1 char. default.

---



 - operation-stack-based, so that the raw, original text is
 stored separately and the operations are executed one after another.
 it makes it so that multiple changes can be experimented with.

 - make margins not a slider control but sliding controls at the
 top of the text area in a separate canvas control aligned with it,
 with combined rule.  it has left and right margin lines and they
 draw them inside the text area.

 - feature (for justified): 
 block shadow: [grayscale level] [depth] [direction] [distance from text]

 - feature (for justified):
 corner brackets: [topleft [], topright [], bottomright [], bottomleft [] ]
 style: [cross | right-angle]
 distance from text: [1 def.]
 
 - left margin solid block. [width: 1 - 10 char.]  [grayscale level]
